REI Kash · Feb 2023 – Jul 2024

End-to-end CRM and customer onboarding

Executive & Back-End Support · US real estate wholesaling

What I owned

  • CRM management: contact records, pipeline hygiene, and segmentation.
  • New customer onboarding from sign-up through first call.
  • Weekly newsletter writing and scheduling.
  • Live support coverage for existing customers.

Tools & systems

  • SalesPype CRM for contact, pipeline, and automation.
  • GoHighLevel for newsletters and campaign workflows.
  • Zoom and Google Meet for live support sessions.
  • Google Workspace and ClickUp for documentation and tasks.
